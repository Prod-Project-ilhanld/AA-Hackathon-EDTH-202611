On board, every drone

Five rules every drone follows on its own

01

No strike without confirmation

A drone fires only once a scout has confirmed the target. If the confirmation is late, it waits.

02

Attack together, or not at all

If one drone of an attack group pulls out, the others pull out too. Never a half-done attack.

03

Stop before a no-fly zone

As soon as a drone spots one on its path, or hears about it from a teammate.

04

Keep your distance

Never closer than 40 metres to another drone.

05

Never hit a friend

If a target turns out to be friendly, the drone stops well before it.

Source: the on-board software of our final submission.
