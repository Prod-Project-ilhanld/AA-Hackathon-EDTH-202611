On board, every drone

Five rules the policy never breaks

01

Never fire without a confirmed observation

R4 guard: a striker waits short of its target, up to 40 s, for a late scout observation.

02

A strike group aborts together

If one co-striker aborts, the others abort too: never half-flown.

03

Hold when a known zone crosses the route

Sensed within 300 m, or heard from a peer one step later.

04

Keep 40 m separation

Hold or back off when a peer is predicted to pass too close (R8).

05

Stop 30 s before a target that turned friendly

Engaging a reclassified target is a failed run.

Source: handin/policy.py at handin-final (R4 guard, group abort, known-zone check, separation hold, STOP_S = 30 s).
