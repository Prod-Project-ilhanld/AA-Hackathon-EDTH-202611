The problem

When the radio dies, each drone is on its own

No ground control

Mid-mission, the link to the operators is cut.

Short sight

Each drone only sees what is close by.

Few words

Drones pass each other tiny messages, and they arrive late.

Surprises

A no-fly zone appears, a target turns friendly, a drone goes down.

One mistake, and the whole mission counts for nothing.

Source: the organisers’ challenge rules for losing the radio link.
