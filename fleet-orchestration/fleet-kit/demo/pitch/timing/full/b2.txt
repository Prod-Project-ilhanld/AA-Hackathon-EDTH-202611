A striker may only fire after a scout has watched and confirmed the target. Some targets need three or four strikers hitting within the same twenty seconds. Each striker flies once. All of them leave one base, stay forty metres apart, and never strike within two hundred metres of a civilian or a friendly. Every target has a time window, every drone a battery. And when the radio link dies, each drone decides alone. Plan it the simple way, one target at a time, and most targets go unserved.
