The problem

What 100 drones must get right, together

01

Scout first

A striker fires only after a scout has watched the target for 20 s and confirmed it, at least 10 s before.

02

Strike together

Some targets need 3 or 4 strikers hitting within the same 20 seconds, or not at all.

03

One shot each

Each striker flies once: its flight ends in its strike. Every one has to count.

04

One base, 40 m apart

All 100 drones take off from the same base and must never come within 40 m of each other.

05

Never near civilians

No strike within 200 m of a civilian or a friendly. Every target has a time window, every drone a battery.

06

Then the link dies

Each drone decides alone, with 64-byte messages, while new dangers appear.

Rules from the challenge kit · SUB-15 Fleet Orchestration · EDTH Paris 2026

“A striker may only fire after a scout has watched and confirmed the target. Some targets need three or four strikers hitting within the same twenty seconds. Each striker flies once. All of them leave one base, stay forty metres apart, and never strike within two hundred metres of a civilian or a friendly. Every target has a time window, every drone a battery. And when the radio link dies, each drone decides alone.”
