500 aircraft?The search budget shrinks to 22 s and the safety net always writes a valid plan. The final version was not re-timed at 500, so we claim no ratio there.
Why not ML?Learned solvers need a GPU, ignore synchronised strikes and the 40 m separation, and can’t say why they chose a target.
Link-loss weak spot?Scenario L3-6: retention 0.611. Two aircraft abort just after take-off and hover over the base. The fix broke three other scenarios, so it is not in.
