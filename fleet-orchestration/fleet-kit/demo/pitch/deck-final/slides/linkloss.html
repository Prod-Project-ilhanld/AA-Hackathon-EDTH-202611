Bonus · when comms are cut

When comms fail, our drones get more careful, not more dangerous

1

The radio link is cut mid-mission. The drones carry on with the plan, alone.

2

A target turns out to be friendly, or a new no-fly zone appears.

3

The drones that see it warn the others with tiny messages.

4

The drones concerned call off their strike together.

“One bonus: when the radio is cut, the drones carry on alone. If a target turns friendly or a no-fly zone appears, they warn each other and stop. When comms fail, our drones get more careful, not more dangerous.” No link-loss figures on stage or in answers for now (PRs #19 and #23 not merged yet).
